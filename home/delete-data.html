Delete User and User Data

To delete all user data and your account:

  1. Open the app and click on the menu in the upper left.
  2. Select Preferences.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and click the ‘Delete all your data’ button.
  4. Follow prompts and you will be logged off and all your user data will be removed.

This will delete your account, as well as your contacts, your club memberships and all of your stored books, movies / TV and podcasts. If you have sent club recommendations to other people, they will still have access to those recommendations. If you sent direct recommendations to people, they will still have those recommendations.
